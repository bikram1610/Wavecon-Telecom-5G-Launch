# Wavecon-Telecom-5G-Launch

📊 Business Problem

Wavecon, a telecom company, launched its 5G services and wanted to understand:

🔹 How did the 5G launch impact overall revenue?
🔹 Which KPIs improved or declined after the launch?
🔹 Which plans are performing well and which are underperforming?
🔹 Were any plans significantly affected or discontinued?
🔹 Which cities are showing positive or negative performance?

🔍 Key Insights

After analyzing the data and building an interactive Power BI dashboard, some important findings were:

📈 ARPU increased by around 11%, indicating higher revenue generated per active user.

📉 Active users decreased by around 8%, while unsubscribed users increased by around 23%.

💰 Overall revenue remained almost flat, suggesting that higher ARPU was partly offset by the decline in the customer base.

⚠️ Plan P7 experienced a significant revenue decline, making it an important plan for further investigation and customer migration analysis.

📱 P1, P11 and P12 showed stronger performance, indicating potential opportunities for further focus.

🏙️ The analysis also showed that city-level performance varied, highlighting the need for localized strategies rather than a single approach for all markets.

💡 Business Recommendation

Based on the analysis, I proposed a simple strategy:

RETAIN → MIGRATE → SCALE

➡️ Retain customers who are at risk of churning
➡️ Migrate customers from weaker/legacy plans to suitable 5G plans
➡️ Scale the better-performing plans and markets

This project helped me understand how Power BI can be used not just to create dashboards, but to translate data into business insights and actionable recommendations.
